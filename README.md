About Us, ## About Us

*P. S. Raj Shipping Enterprises* is an established and professionally managed marine repair and ship maintenance company with over *23 years of industry experience*. Based in Mumbai, we provide reliable marine repair, ship maintenance, tank cleaning, surface preparation, painting, and skilled manpower solutions to the shipping and offshore industries.

With a strong understanding of the demanding requirements of the marine sector, we undertake projects across ports, shipyards, dry docks, anchorages, and offshore locations. Our experienced workforce and practical expertise enable us to execute projects efficiently while maintaining high standards of *quality, safety, workmanship, and timely completion*.

Our core services include *tank cleaning, hull cleaning, chipping, painting, high-pressure and low-pressure jet washing, hydro blasting, grit/copper slag blasting, surface preparation, tank coating, stencil/name painting, and skilled manpower supply. We also provide experienced marine personnel including **Chief Engineers, Technical Superintendents, Mechanical Engineers, supervisors, skilled workers, and other project manpower* as per client requirements.

We are *ISO 9001:2015 and ABS certified*, reflecting our commitment to structured quality management and professional working practices. Our experience includes working with leading shipping and offshore organizations, and we have successfully executed projects at major Indian ports and maritime facilities.

At P. S. Raj Shipping Enterprises, we believe that every project is built on *trust, responsibility, quality, and performance*. Our objective is not simply to complete a job, but to become a dependable long-term service partner for our clients.
